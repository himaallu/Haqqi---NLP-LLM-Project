# Labelling guide

## General
- Label from the question only, never from the lawyers' answers.
- One problem type per question. Explain any doubt in Notes.

## problem_type
- unpaid_wages: salary not paid, paid late, or paid only in part (including overtime and allowances).
- passport_withheld: employer keeps the passport, Emirates ID or other official documents.
- gratuity: end-of-service gratuity not paid, or a question about how much is owed.
- unfair_termination: dismissed without valid reason or without notice, or asking for compensation for dismissal.
- recruitment_fees: worker charged recruitment, visa or hiring costs by the employer or an agency.
- court_enforcement: the person already has a labour case, judgment or MOHRE decision and asks about the court process: enforcing or collecting a judgment, appeals, court fees, hearings, evidence, or amending or withdrawing a claim.
- other: anything else. Write a short topic in Notes (for example "visa ban", "annual leave", "notice period", "resignation").

Several problems: choose the one the person is mainly asking about. If equal, choose the one mentioned first. Write the other in Notes as "also: <type>".

Checkpoint: after 100 labels, review the Notes of "other". If one topic appears often, add it as a new type and relabel those.

## asker
- worker: asking about their own job.
- employer: an employer, manager, HR or sponsor asking about an employee.
- unclear: cannot tell.

Domestic workers (maids, drivers, nannies hired by a household) are covered by a separate law, not Decree-Law 33/2021. Label them normally and write "domestic worker" in Notes.

## Facts (copy exactly as written; leave empty if not stated)
- amount: the money in dispute (salary, amount owed, gratuity), e.g. "AED 5,000".
- date: date of the key event (termination, last salary, resignation), e.g. "July 2024".
- duration: how long the person worked for this employer, e.g. "4 years". Put other periods ("unpaid for 3 months") in Notes.
- employer: the employer's actual name only; leave empty for "my company".

## Notes tags
- "events pre2022": the situation happened under the old law.
- "domestic worker": see above.
- "also: <type>": a second problem.
- "PII": a personal name is left in the text.
